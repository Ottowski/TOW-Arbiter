# Warhammer: The Old World - Forming Units

## Formation Types

All units must adopt a formation, which affects how they move and fight. A unit’s available formation types are granted by special rules of the same name. Models with multiple formation-type rules choose one at deployment and may change it later by reforming. Close Order is the most common and is the focus of core play; other formations such as Skirmish and Open Order appear in advanced rules or specific army lists.

## Close Order Formation

A unit in Close Order consists of two or more models arranged in base contact, edge-to-edge and front corner to front corner. All models face the same direction and are arranged into ranks (horizontal rows) and files (vertical columns), i.e., rank-and-file. As far as possible, ranks should contain the same number of models; if not possible, the rear rank has fewer models. Once formed, the unit moves and fights as a single entity.

## Unit Shape (Close Order)

Close Order units gain certain bonuses depending on their shape. Such units are either in Combat Order or in Marching Column.

## Combat Order (Close Order)

A Close Order unit that is wider than it is deep (more models per rank than per file) or square (same models per rank and file) is in Combat Order.

Rank Bonus: If wide enough, a unit in Close Order can claim +1 combat result point for each full rank behind the first, up to the maximum allowed by its troop type. How many models are required per rank and the maximum rank bonus depend on the troop type. Certain special rules may increase the maximum. An incomplete rear rank can still count if it has sufficient models. Disrupted units cannot claim a rank bonus.
Combat Result Bonus: While in Combat Order, a Close Order unit with Unit Strength 10 or more may claim an additional +1 combat result point.

## Marching Column

A unit that is deeper than it is wide (more models per file than per rank) is in Marching Column. A Marching Column cannot claim a Rank Bonus, cannot declare a charge, and may triple its Movement characteristic when Marching.
